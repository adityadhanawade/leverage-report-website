# Phase 2 — Research
### The Leverage Report — Web App

> **Research** in UI/UX means understanding two things before you design: (1) **WHO** you're
> designing for (a "user persona" — a realistic profile of your typical visitor), and (2)
> **WHAT already exists** (a "competitor analysis" — similar things, what's good/bad about them).
> This stops you designing for yourself and grounds every later decision in real user needs.
> Recruiters ask "who was your user?" — this document is your answer. Portfolio artifact.

## PART A — User Personas (Proto-Personas)

> A persona is a *made-up but realistic* person who represents a group of your real visitors.
> We give them a name and face (mentally) so that during design we can ask "would Emma
> understand this button?" instead of guessing. We'll make TWO: a primary and a secondary.

> **⚠️ HONESTY NOTE (important — this is the truthful method, and the answer to give recruiters):**
> These are **proto-personas** — i.e. *assumption-based* personas, NOT built from in-person
> interviews with Western users. That is a standard, legitimate professional method for a solo
> project. They are grounded in **real signal**: the brand's actual Instagram audience is Western
> (US/UK/CA/AU) per the page's audience data, so a Western target user is accurate — the personas
> describe *the user*, not the designer (who is based in India). **No claim is ever made of
> traveling or interviewing people abroad.**
>
> **How to upgrade them later (optional, even more impressive):** validate with a quick survey/poll
> to real Instagram followers, or a few DM conversations, then revise → this turns "proto-personas"
> into "research-based personas." Recommended if time allows; not required for v1.
>
> **Recruiter script:** *"My target market is Western, based on my real audience analytics. I built
> assumption-based proto-personas from that data to guide design, and I was transparent that they
> aren't from primary interviews — with more time I'd validate them with user surveys."*

### 👤 Primary Persona — "Emma, the Money-Conscious Professional"

| | |
|---|---|
| **Age / role** | 28, marketing coordinator |
| **Location** | Manchester, UK (Western audience) |
| **Tech comfort** | Medium — uses apps daily, has tried ChatGPT a couple of times |
| **Income** | Decent salary but feels money "leaks away" each month |

**Goals (what she wants):**
- Stop wasting money on subscriptions she forgot about.
- Feel more confident asking for a raise.
- Use AI to save time/money but doesn't know the right way to prompt it.

**Frustrations (pain points):**
- "Everyone says use AI for money but nobody shows the *exact* steps."
- Money advice online is either fluff, ads, or feels like a scam.
- She saved a helpful Instagram reel once and could never find it again.

**How she finds the site:** taps the link in the Instagram bio after watching a reel.

**What success looks like for her:** in under 2 minutes she gets one real, usable thing
(a copied prompt, or seeing she wastes $240/yr on unused subscriptions) — for free, no signup.

## PART B — Competitor / Landscape Analysis

> "Competitors" doesn't mean enemies — it means anything a visitor might use *instead* of
> your site. We study them to (a) copy what works and (b) find the gap we can own.

| Type of site | What's GOOD about it | What's BAD about it | What WE do differently |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Generic "1000 ChatGPT prompts" sites** | Lots of prompts, free | Overwhelming, untested, no niche, ad-heavy, no honesty | Curated, *tested*, money-focused, "the catch" stated |
| **Personal-finance calculator/blog sites** | Useful calculators | Cluttered with ads, generic, not AI-focused, dated design | Clean, no ads, AI + money angle, modern design |
| **Linktree-style bio pages** | Simple, quick to set up | Just links — *zero* actual value to the visitor | Real interactive tools, not just a list of links |
| **AI money influencers' pages** | Good reach/trust | Value trapped in videos; nothing to *use*, often upsell paid courses | Free usable tools, never upsell, honest |

**The gap we own:** *a clean, honest, free site with actual working AI-money tools you can
use in 2 minutes — not a link list, not an ad-farm, not a course funnel.*

---

## PART C — Key research takeaways (these guide the design)
1. **Speed to value is everything** — Emma must get something useful in <2 min, no signup wall.
2. **Trust through honesty** — visibly state the "catch"; no ads, no upsell. This is the brand edge.
3. **Findability** — the thing she lost on Instagram must be easy to find again here (search).
4. **Two moods to serve** — "save my money" (Emma) and "earn more / learn" (Ryan).
5. **Clean, modern, uncluttered** — the competition looks dated/ad-heavy; looking clean *is* a feature.
