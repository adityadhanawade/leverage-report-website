## 1. One-line summary
A free web app — the "AI-Money Toolkit" — that gives people simple, honest, interactive
tools to make, save, and grow money using AI. Built under the brand *The Leverage Report*.

## 2. Background / context
The brand already exists as an Instagram page (@the.leverage.report) posting short videos
about AI + money to a Western audience. Instagram is temporary and unsearchable: a tip
scrolls by and is gone. The website becomes the brand's **permanent home** — where every
tip turns into a usable tool people can return to, search, and trust.

## 3. Problem statement
People hear that "AI can help you with money," but:
- they don't know the *exact* steps or wording to actually do it,
- useful tips disappear in the social-media feed,
- most "money" sites are full of fluff, ads, or push sketchy paid products.

**There's no simple, honest, free place that turns "AI can help your money" into tools you
can use in two minutes.** That's the gap this site fills.

## 4. Goals & objectives
**User goals (what a visitor wants):**
- Get a genuinely useful result fast, for free, with no signup wall.
- Trust that it's honest (the "catch" is always stated).

**Owner goals (what you want):**
- Give the Instagram audience a real reason to visit (a proper "link in bio").
- Build a standout **portfolio piece** — one project that proves both **UI/UX design** and
  **full-stack development** skill to recruiters.
- Keep an audience channel you *own* (email), independent of Instagram.

## 5. Target audience ✅ (confirmed 2026-07-25)
Primary: people who follow / discover the Instagram page — broadly, everyday people
interested in using AI to improve their money situation (subscriptions, salary, savings,
freelancing). Detailed persona comes in Phase 2.
- **Geography: Western audience (US / UK / Canada / Australia).** ✅ Confirmed.
- **Currency shown in tools: US dollars ($)** as primary. Tone/spelling: US English.
- Note: the site is *built for* a Western audience but is *shown to* recruiters in India as
  the owner's portfolio work — designing for an international market is itself a resume plus.

## 6. Success metrics (how we'll know it worked) ⚠️ (confirm/adjust)
- People actually *use* the tools (clicks on "Copy" / "Calculate"), not just visit.
- Visitors arrive from the Instagram bio link.
- Some visitors join the email list.
- (Portfolio metric) A live, deployed URL + a written case study you can show recruiters.

## 7. Scope
**In scope (v1 — the 8 deliverables):**
1. Homepage  2. AI Prompt Tool ⭐  3. Subscription-Leak Calculator  4. Savings Goal Planner
5. Raise/Negotiation Builder  6. Prompt Library  7. Message Templates  8. AI Terms Glossary
+ Weekly Email signup.

**Out of scope (v1 — deliberately NOT doing now):**
- User accounts / logins.
- Paid features or payments.
- A live server-side AI (the Prompt Tool uses free in-browser templates for v1).
- A blog with many articles (the Tactic Archive can come later).

## 8. Constraints & assumptions
- **Budget: ₹0.** Free stack, free hosting, free tiers only. (Free `*.vercel.app` address.)
- **Legit only.** Never recommend illegal/pirated/sketchy tools.
- **Anonymous brand.** No personal name or info on the public site.
- **Tech:** Next.js + React + TypeScript; design in Figma; deploy on Vercel.
- Assumption: traffic is small at first (fits every free tier comfortably).

## 9. Deliverables of this project
- UI/UX: Figma wireframes, design system, high-fidelity mockups, clickable prototype.
- Engineering: the coded, responsive, deployed website.
- Documentation: this brief, per-phase artifacts, and a final **case study**.

## 10. High-level plan
The 10-phase process in PROJECT.md (Discovery → Research → IA → Wireframes → Design System
→ Mockups → Prototype → Test → Development → Deploy), followed by the case study.
